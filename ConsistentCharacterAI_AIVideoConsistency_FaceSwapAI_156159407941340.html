📋 Case Study 1: YouTube True Crime Channel — 500K Subscribers

📌 The Challenge

A true crime YouTube channel with 500K subscribers wanted to expand into Spanish-speaking markets. Their auto-dubbed videos using standard AI tools showed a 29% drop in retention compared to English originals. They were losing 50% of their potential international audience.

🔧 The Solution

Applied the 4 Pillars Framework with a focus on suspense preservation:

  • Step 1: Mapped every suspense peak and emotional reveal in the script
  • Step 2: Cloned the host's voice with emotional intensity mapping (suspense = lower pitch, measured tempo)
  • Step 3: Cultural transcreation for Spanish audiences (adjusted suspense-building techniques)
  • Step 4: Manual lip-sync refinement on all emotional keyframes

📌 The Challenge
