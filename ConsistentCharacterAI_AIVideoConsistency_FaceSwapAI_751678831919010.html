🧠 The Neuroscience Behind the Results

1. The 100ms Trust Rule

The brain decides whether to trust a face in 100 milliseconds—before conscious awareness. Hyper-realistic avatars avoid triggering the "uncanny valley" response, allowing trust to build naturally.

2. Emotional Mirroring

When a viewer sees a face expressing an emotion (pain, joy, relief), their brain's mirror neurons replicate that emotion. This is why micro-expressions are critical—they create empathy and connection.

3. The Scarcity Effect

CTAs that create urgency activate the brain's loss aversion system. The fear of missing out (FOMO) is a stronger driver than the desire for gain. This is why CTAs like "Only 5 left" or "Limited time" outperform generic CTAs.

4. Social Proof Bias

The brain uses social proof as a mental shortcut. When an AI avatar says "8,000+ customers already use this," the viewer's brain processes it as "if others trust this, I can too."
