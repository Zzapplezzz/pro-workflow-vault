📦 The 10-Minute Template System

The secret to speed is pre-built templates. Instead of starting from scratch every time, you create a library of reusable components.

Template #1: The Hook Bank

Create a document with 20 pre-written hooks organized by emotion and product type.

  • Curiosity: "I can't believe I didn't discover this sooner..."
  • Urgency: "This deal won't last — here's why."
  • Social Proof: "Everyone I know is switching to this."
  • Pain Point: "Tired of spending €500 on ads that don't convert?"

Time saved: 15 minutes per video.

Template #2: The Avatar Library

Pre-select 3-5 avatars that match your brand persona. Save their settings (appearance, voice, language).

  • Avatar A: Young female, energetic, for beauty/fashion
  • Avatar B: Middle-aged male, authoritative, for B2B/finance
  • Avatar C: Young male, casual, for tech/gaming

Time saved: 10 minutes per video.

Template #3: The Montage Preset

Create video editing presets in your software of choice (CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci).

  • Preset A: Fast-paced with dynamic subtitles (for TikTok)
  • Preset B: Professional with smooth transitions (for Meta/YouTube)
  • Preset C: Minimalist with slow pacing (for explainers)

Time saved: 20 minutes per video.

📊 The Accelerated Workflow (Step-by-Step)

  1. Script (5 min): Copy-paste from your Hook Bank + fill in product details.
  2. Avatar Generation (5 min): Paste script into your AI tool with a pre-selected avatar.
  3. Download (5 min): Export the raw AI video.
  4. Montage (20 min): Apply your preset — subtitles, music, branding, transitions.
  5. Export (5 min): Render and save in the correct format.
  6. Review (10 min): Watch on mobile and desktop, make minor tweaks.

Total time: ~50 minutes. Quality: Professional-grade, ready for ads.

🧠 Why Speed Matters for Performance

Here's a neuroscience insight most people miss: speed of iteration is more important than perfection.

  • The 7-Day Creative Decay: Ad performance drops 50% after 7 days. You need fresh content weekly.
  • The Law of Diminishing Returns: Spending 4 hours on one video instead of 1 hour only improves conversion by ~10%.
  • Testing Volume Wins: 10 videos tested rapidly will outperform 2 "perfect" videos every time.

This is why the accelerated workflow is non-negotiable for serious advertisers.
